
## Teaching / Learning

*Programming is decision-making under constraints.*

Teacher focus:
- Trade-offs
- No universal "best practice"
- Context matters

Student tasks:
- Write short design rationales
- Defend decisions orally or in writing

### Example ..

Given ambiguous requirements:
- Propose two designs
- Explicitly list risks and assumptions

LLM use:
- Generate alternative designs
- Students choose and justify one
