
## Algorithms in General

The document *"[Algorithms: The Complete Series](./algorithms_series.pdf)"* is
designed to provide a structured overview of selected developments in algorithms,
presented from the perspective of a large language model (Claude). Its purpose
is not to serve as an exhaustive or authoritative reference, but rather to map
out important themes, methods, and directions within the field.

Although the title suggests completeness, the series is perhaps intentionally
selective. Some algorithms are discussed in earlier sections of the book/repo,
others appear later if the text is read sequentially, and certain topics are
only mentioned in passing or omitted entirely (the biological part). For example,
areas such as blockchain technology are not explored in depth at all. So the
"complete" aspect should therefore be understood as conceptual rather than
encyclopedic.

In addition to surveying algorithmic ideas, the document also implicitly
illustrates the capabilities of contemporary large language models (circa
early 2026), including their generative scope and confidence calibration
shaped by reinforcement learning processes. I guess.

The PDF is intended for flexible use: individual study, group discussion,
or even classroom settings. Its primary aim is to stimulate curiosity, suggest
avenues for deeper exploration, and provide a coherent entry point into
the broad and evolving landscape of algorithmic theory and practice.
Rather than closing the subject, it is meant to open it.
