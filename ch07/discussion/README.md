
## Teaching / Learning

*Correctness lives in details.*

Teacher focus:
- Memory, concurrency, failure
- "Works on my machine" is not success

Student tasks:
- Identify race conditions
- Reason about lifetimes and ownership

### Example ..

Provide a concurrent program that:
- works most of the time
- fails under stress

Students must:
- Reproduce failure
- Explain root cause
- Fix minimally

LLM use:
- Treated as an unreliable reviewer
- Students must disagree with it at least once
